# Pitcar Academy

Landing pages and checkout for Pitcar Academy's online and offline classes, and the LMS behind them.

## Harga kelas online

**Harga jual**:
What a class costs today, bought on its own; the price the LMS admin sets.
_Avoid_: harga satuan, harga launching (that is a label, not a price)

**Harga coret**:
A higher price shown struck through next to the harga jual. It counts only when it is above the harga jual.
_Avoid_: strike price, harga asli

**Harga normal**:
The price a line is measured against: its harga coret when it has one, otherwise its harga jual. The same rule holds for the class being bought and for every tambahan bundling.

**Hemat**:
Harga normal minus what is charged. Never negative.
_Avoid_: diskon, potongan, savings

**Tambahan bundling**:
Another class offered under the order bump at checkout, for less than its harga jual and only in that checkout.
_Avoid_: add-on, upsell

**Harga tawaran**:
What a tambahan bundling costs when ticked at checkout.
_Avoid_: offer price, harga bundling

**Rincian harga**:
The breakdown under the order bump: each line's harga normal struck through and what is charged, then the totals, the hemat and the total bayar.
_Avoid_: receipt, quote, ringkasan
